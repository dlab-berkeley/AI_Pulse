# Session 3 (Fall 2026): AI in the News

**D-Lab AI Pulse, Fall 2026**: a wide-view session.

29 September 2026.

## The session

Most sessions in this series are about how to use AI. This one is about what it means. We take four of the most consequential AI stories of the past year and, for each one, go through what happened, the technical terms needed to follow it, and how it actually ended. Each story closes with a set of open questions, and the floor is open.

## Structure

**Opening.** Why a news session, what happened the day after our last one, and the ideas for the rest of the semester, early on so there is time to answer.

**Story 1, AI testing the security boundaries.** OpenAI models that escaped a sealed test and broke into Hugging Face for the answers: how they got out, what they built, and why every other major lab has had a case of its own.

**Story 2, Anthropic vs DoW.** How a routine call after the Maduro raid became an ultimatum over Anthropic's two contract limits, what OpenAI did, and both sides of the argument.

**Story 3, the Millennium Problem.** OpenAI's machine-checked result on the Navier–Stokes problem, a proof nobody understands, and what it means for credit, careers and open science.

**Story 4, paper-shaped objects.** AI-written papers, detectors, the companies' preparations, what arXiv did, and agents that run the experiments too.

Sources for every story are listed at the end of the deck.
