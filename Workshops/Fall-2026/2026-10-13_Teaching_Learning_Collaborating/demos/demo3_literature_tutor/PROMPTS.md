# Demo 3: A tutor for a new literature

**Notebook:** six papers (in the Spring demo9 folder): Chetty et al. 2022 Social Capital I; Chetty, Friedman, Saez, Turner & Yagan 2020 (income segregation and colleges); Chetty, Deming & Friedman 2023 (diversifying society's leaders); Chetty, Hendren, Jones & Porter 2020 (race and economic opportunity); Chetty et al. 2024 (changing opportunity); Jacome, Kuziemko & Naidu 2021 (mobility for all).
**Configure Chat:** Learning Guide. **Share:** none needed.

## Prompts
1. `I'm new to this literature. How do these six papers build on each other, and in what order were they written?`
2. `Which of these papers measure the same thing in different ways?`
3. `Where do two of these papers seem to disagree, and what explains the difference?`
4. `Explain economic connectedness to me, then check whether I understood it.` (answer its question, badly on purpose once)

## Studio
- Mind Map (all six).
- Quiz on Social Capital I only (select one source).
- Audio Overview, Debate format: "Is exposure or friending bias the bigger barrier?"
