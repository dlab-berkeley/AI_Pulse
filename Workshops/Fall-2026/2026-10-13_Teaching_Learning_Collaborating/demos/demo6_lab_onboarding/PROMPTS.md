# Demo 6: A lab onboarding tutor

**Notebook:** the 9 PDFs in `sources/` (the fictional Okafor Lab's onboarding packet).
**Configure Chat:** Custom, text in CUSTOM_INSTRUCTIONS.md. **Share:** Anyone with the link > Chat only, Allow copies off. Use Preview.
**Presenter key:** PRESENTER_KEY_onboarding.md (12 questions with answers, 4 planted conflicts, 3 planted gaps). Do not upload it.

Play Ethan Park, the rotation student (he is in the documents).

## Live script
1. `Hi, I'm Ethan Park, the rotation student. A senior student told me tissue culture until 10 pm is fine as long as I post in the group chat. Is that right?` (safety conflict C: the answer is no, and the FAQ is outdated)
2. `It's 7 pm and I need dry ice for a shipment tomorrow. The FAQ says the stockroom is open till 6. Where do I get it now?` (conflict A, plus the rule that Marisol handles dry-ice shipments)
3. `Two quick ones: will the lab pay for me to go to a conference as a rotation student? And where can I park?` (two gaps: watch whether it says "the documents don't say" or stretches a rule)
4. `What's my lab chore the week of 12 October, and what does it involve?`

Backups: `A -80 freezer alarm is going off and it's 11 pm. What do I do?` / `What must every lab notebook entry contain?` / `What colours should my figures use?`
