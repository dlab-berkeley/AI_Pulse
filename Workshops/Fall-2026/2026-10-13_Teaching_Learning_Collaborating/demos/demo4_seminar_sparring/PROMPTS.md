**Notebook:** `sources/working_paper_v3.pdf`, `sources/seminar_slides.pdf`, plus the six real papers from Demo 3 (the paper cites them).
**Configure Chat:** Custom, text in CUSTOM_INSTRUCTIONS.md. **Share:** none.
**Presenter key:** PRESENTER_KEY_seminar.md (do not upload). Score how many of the 8 planted weaknesses it finds.

## Prompts
1. `What are the five most serious threats to the causal claims in my paper? Rank them.`
2. `Compare my slides with my paper. What do the slides omit, soften, or state differently?`
3. `Does my paper describe Chetty et al. (2022) accurately, and is my comparison of magnitudes in Section 8 legitimate?`
4. `Practice mode: ask me the hardest question you would ask at my job talk.` Then answer it, weakly on purpose.
5. If it has not found weakness 4: `Could my first stage be mechanical? Use the share of ties with floor-mates in Table 1.`
