# Session 2 (Fall 2026): The Major AI Ecosystems

**D-Lab AI Pulse, Fall 2026** — the hands-on counterpart to Session 1.

Delivered 15 September 2026.

## The session

Session 1 was the map. This one opens the tools. We run the same kinds of research task through Anthropic's environment and OpenAI's, side by side, and look at where they actually diverge, which turns out to be almost nowhere near where the marketing points.

## Structure

**Part 1, the basics.** Model lineups, what the plans cost, what each plan actually includes, how usage is counted, and the academic programmes worth applying to. The free tiers are asymmetric in a way that shapes what you can follow along with.

**Part 2, what they have in common.** Projects and their capacity limits, memory, and why most of the feature list is not a reason to choose either one.

**Part 3, where they differ in practice.** Artifacts against Canvas, file creation, coding agents, knowledge-work agents, connectors, scheduled jobs, and packaging a repeatable job as a skill.

**Part 4, what you are agreeing to.** Training defaults on personal accounts, what deletion does and does not do, text watermarking, and what only one side has.

Running through all four: one task carried end to end, which is how to keep up with AI.
