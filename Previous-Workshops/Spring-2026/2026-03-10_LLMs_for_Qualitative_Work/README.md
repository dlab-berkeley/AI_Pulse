# LLMs for Qualitative Work

D-Lab AI Pulse Workshop, Session 4, March 10, 2026

AI tools have transformed quantitative research workflows, but qualitative researchers have been largely left out of the conversation. This session explored how LLMs fit into qualitative work through five live demos: grounded document analysis with **NotebookLM**, dialogical qualitative coding, multimodal analysis of photos and video, structured text extraction from open-ended responses, and piloting research designs with simulated participants. We also discussed the unique risks LLMs pose for interpretive work and where human judgment remains essential in the analysis loop.

## Key Files

- `slides/workshop_slides.pdf`: Compiled slides
- `demos/`: Five demo modules with prompts, sample data, and dry-run outputs:
  - `module1_document_analysis/`: Grounded document analysis with NotebookLM
  - `module2_qualitative_coding/`: Dialogical qualitative coding from interview transcripts
  - `module3_multimodal_analysis/`: Image-based theme analysis
  - `module4_structured_extraction/`: LangExtract for survey response coding
  - `module5_simulated_participants/`: Piloting designs with simulated participants
