# AI for Teaching, Learning and Collaborating

D-Lab AI Pulse Workshop, Session 3, February 24, 2026

How can AI help you learn, teach, and work with others? This session explored **NotebookLM**, Google's source-grounded AI that generates podcasts, flashcards, study guides, and more from your own documents. Answers come from your sources, not the internet. We walked through 9 live demos covering exam prep, literature synthesis, course material creation, policy navigation, and collaborative research workflows. We also touched on **Khanmigo**, **Microsoft Study**, and **SciSpace**, and discussed the rise of AI homework agents, AI humanizers, and what they mean for assessments.
