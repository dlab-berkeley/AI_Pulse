# Scientific AI

D-Lab AI Pulse Workshop, Session 2, February 10, 2026

## Workshop Arc

1. **The Problem**: why general-purpose AI hallucinates citations, with real-world consequences from law, consulting, and academia.
2. **The Solution**: how specialized research tools (Perplexity, Consensus, Elicit) retrieve from verified sources instead of generating from patterns.
3. **The Frontier**: Kosmos, an autonomous research agent that reads ~1,500 papers and writes ~42,000 lines of code over 12 hours to produce a research report.
